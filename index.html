Our Story

About Lebohang Wodworking & Upholstery

A proudly South African enterprise built on craftsmanship, integrity and a commitment to quality that speaks for itself.

Our Background

From Limpopo, For Limpopo

Lebohang Wodworking and Upholstery (Pty) Ltd was established in 2017 in Ga Sekororo, Limpopo. Founded with a vision to provide world-class craftsmanship at the local level, we have grown into a trusted name in wodworking, upholstery and construction contracting.

As a 100% Black-Owned business, we are deeply invested in the communities we serve. Our work is not just about building furniture or completing construction projects — it is about building futures, creating employment and contributing to economic transformation in Limpopo.

We hold full formal registration, including CIPC, CIDB and B-BBEE Level 1 certification — making us a preferred partner for both private clients and government procurement processes.
